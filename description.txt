<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">With their hard-rocking rhythms, dual drummer power, and tight three-part vocal harmonies,</span> The Doobie Brothers have been a mainstay in the rock &amp; roll landscape for nearly five decades.</h2></div>

<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">Biggie Smalls aka The Notorious B.I.G.</span> prophesied his short but impactful career with albums “Ready to Die” and “Life After Death” – two of hip-hop’s most influential albums.</h2></div>

<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">The Go-Go’s catchy, well-crafted songs</span> formed a bridge between the brash urgency of L.A. punk and the dark melodies of new wave pop. </h2></div>

<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">The Spinners fused Motown roots with a Philly soul sound, thriving on both the R&amp;B and pop charts.</span> With producer/songwriter Thom Bell, the quintet perfected a blend of silky harmony, dance grooves, lush orchestration and, most importantly, classic, unforgettable songs with emotional depth. Bell wrote for their distinctive voices, as individuals and a fivesome, helping them score hit after ‘70s hit. </h2></div>

<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">With a style encompassing jazz, soul, R&amp;B and pop,</span> Kool &amp; the Gang brought a distinctive groove to the mainstream, their infectious tunes propelled by horn-laden funk and seamless harmonies. Offering melodic and rhythmic hooks, they’re one of the most sampled acts in hip-hop. Their crossover success influenced artists like Prince and Bruno Mars. The band’s party anthems remain staples at celebratory events. </h2></div>

<div class="Intro_intro__content__zrHuF"><h2 class="Intro_headline__b4Fow title-lc-m"><span class="highlighted-text">The White Stripes reinvigorated rock &amp; roll by returning it to its primal blues roots,</span> proving that a duo with defiant energy, catchy riffs, melodic savvy and unique style could captivate as much, if not more, than a large, complex ensemble. Both live and on record, mastermind multi-instrumentalist and vocalist Jack White and minimalist drummer Meg White crafted a sound greater than the sum of its parts, inspiring artists from multiple genres.</h2></div>
